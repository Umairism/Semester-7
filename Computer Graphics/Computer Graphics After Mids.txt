Drawing and Co-ordinate Systems:
    Drawing on canvas(Device, Whiteboard, Paper, Board)

    Viewport:
        Region on a screen where objects displayed.(can be called as canvas) visible to user.
            Change size, position.
    Ortho 2D:
        How we are displaying out or seeing the object. Projection or Displaying 2D objects on screen, Angle of seeing the 2D object.

Co-ordinate System:
    - World Window  -> specific region from real world that we are going to plot on canvas. selected area for the display.
    - World Co-ordinate system  -> x1 y1 cordinates of that specific objects.(l,r,b,t)
    - Viewport  -> area of display device to what to be displayed.
    - Viewport (Screen) co-ordinate system  ->Sx Sy cordinates on the canvas.(L,B,W,H)
    - Window to Viewport mapping.
